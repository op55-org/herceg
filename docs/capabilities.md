## Placement

- **Named panel**: The row's mark drawn in one of two panels of one size draws what it draws in the other; the line beside it draws as alone; no other panel draws anything.
- **Facets**: Split by the chart's facet field into panels N and S, each panel draws what the mark draws over that group's rows alone.
- **Facets in a layout cell**: A layout cell splits the mark's rows into facet panels inside it.
- **Nested layout**: In columns of rows of four panels of one size, the mark draws in its panel what it draws in another, at its place in the nesting; the others draw nothing of it.
- **Spanning cell**: In a cell spanning both columns above two panels, the mark draws as in the top panel of rows of a panel and a grid of two: across both columns.
- **Inset**: In one of two insets of one size over a panel, the mark draws what it draws in the other; the panel draws its line as without it.

## Selection

- **Brush x**: Dragging reports the x range dragged (and no y range) as the select event and chart.selection, without a store and so without rows.
- **Brush y**: Dragging reports the y range dragged (and no x range) as the select event and chart.selection, without a store and so without rows.
- **Brush xy**: Dragging reports both ranges dragged as the select event and chart.selection, without a store and so without rows.
- **Lasso**: Drawing a lasso reports its outline as the select event and chart.selection, without a store and so without rows.
- **Rows by x**: On a chart bound to a selection store, the gesture writes exactly the rows the fixture lists to the store's channel, and its select event says so.
- **Rows by y**: On a chart bound to a selection store, the gesture writes exactly the rows the fixture lists to the store's channel, and its select event says so.
- **Rows by xy**: On a chart bound to a selection store, the gesture writes exactly the rows the fixture lists to the store's channel, and its select event says so.
- **Rows by lasso**: On a chart bound to a selection store, the gesture writes exactly the rows the fixture lists to the store's channel, and its select event says so.
- **Click**: Clicking a point, bar, bin, group, cell, or item writes exactly the rows it stands for to the store.
- **Receive**: A chart bound to receive only (events: []) gets the selection another view writes: its rowSelection event reports it, and where the chart highlights, its description counts the rows selected.
- **Highlight**: On Canvas, a selection draws a row it holds as it is and dims a row it does not hold, where the fixture says they are; a selection of no rows dims both, and clearing it draws the mark as before.

## Legends

- **Categorical legend**: The legend lists the mark's categories, in order, each entry's swatch in the color the mark draws it with.
- **Colorbar**: A colorbar naming the mark (guides: colorbar with scale { mark, channel: "color" }), colored by a number field, labels ticks within the field's extent.
- **Entry of your own**: A legend entry of the chart's own naming the mark (targets: [{ mark }]) is listed, and clicking it hides the whole mark and nothing else, and shows it again.
- **Toggle**: Clicking the legend's first entry of the mark hides what it names and nothing else (the chart's hidden keys say so), and clicking it again shows it all.
- **Panel toggle**: With a legend per panel, clicking the first entry of one panel's legend hides it in that panel alone, and clicking it again shows it.

## Reading

- **Nearest x**: With tooltip mode nearest-x, the pointer on a datum reads every series' point at its x: the hover event's points, their labels, rows, and texts, are the fixture's. Annotations of a table along x (rules, text) read their row at its x.
- **Nearest y**: With tooltip mode nearest-y, the pointer on a datum reads every series' point at its y, as the fixture lists them. Annotations of a table along y (regions, text) read their row at its y.
- **Nearest**: With tooltip mode nearest, the pointer on a datum, a bin, a group, a cell, an item, a merge, or an annotation of a table reads that one, as the fixture says; inside a stacked segment, the segment it is in.
- **Tooltip fields**: With tooltip fields naming a field of the table, the pointer on an item that stands for one row (a point, bar, cell, slice, stage, leaf, link, node, merge, sample, or annotation of a table) reads that field of its row too.
- **Keyboard**: Focused, the chart announces what the arrow keys move to, in order: the fixture's first two announcements.
- **Description**: The chart's accessible description names what the mark draws: its series or items and their extent, as the fixture lists.

## Lifecycle

- **View animation**: With animation on, a view asked for moves gradually (setViewport along a continuous axis, a map's or network's view, a treemap's or sunburst's drill): mid-way the mark draws neither where it was nor where it goes, and it ends as a chart without animation draws it.
- **Value animation**: With animation.values, a value changed in place (Table.set) moves the mark gradually: mid-way it draws neither the old value nor the new, and it ends as a chart without animation draws it, inside a layout's panels as alone (a shared proof).
- **Cursor sync**: Two charts in a cursor group: the pointer on one shows the cursor at that value in the other, though it shows another range (its crosshair at the value's pixel), once and without echo; a channel naming a panel carries that panel's cursor only (a shared proof).
- **Viewport sync**: Two charts in a viewport group: a view set on one (setViewport) is the other's at once, along x or, where x shows categories, along y; each reports it once.
- **Selection sync**: Two charts in a selection group: a range selected on one is the other's selection, in data values, reported once on each.
- **Hydration**: A chart drawn on the server (renderToSnapshot) is hydrated: its element and geometry nodes are taken over, not written again; it reads its datum, takes an update, and leaves the page when destroyed.

## Export

- **SVG**: toSVG writes a well-formed SVG document at the chart's size, with the live chart's text, which the browser draws with the mark's colors at every place inside its plots the mark draws at, and its text in its place; exports draw the chart as it is now (a shared proof).
- **PNG**: toPNG writes a PNG of the SVG export at the chart's size (or a dpi's), which shows the mark's colors at every place inside its plots the mark draws at, and its text in its place; exports draw the chart as it is now (a shared proof).
- **PDF**: renderToPDF writes a document qpdf checks clean, whose page MuPDF draws with the mark's colors at every place inside its plots the mark draws at, and its text in its place; MuPDF reads the text the SVG export writes.

## Integration

- **React**: The row's mark element (&lt;Line> ...) in a &lt;Chart> draws what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused option through its onHover and onError props, takes a new prop on the chart it made, and leaves nothing when unmounted.
- **Vue**: The row's mark component in a Vue &lt;Chart> draws what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused option through its hover and error events, takes a new prop on the chart it made, and leaves nothing when unmounted.
- **Svelte**: The row's mark component in a Svelte &lt;Chart> draws what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused option through its onhover and onerror props, takes a new prop on the chart it made, and leaves nothing when unmounted.
- **Notebook widget**: The row's chart sent from Python to the notebook widget draws what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused change back to Python, takes a change of options on the chart it made, and leaves nothing when closed.
- **Streamlit**: The row's chart sent from Python to the Streamlit component draws what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused change, takes new options on the chart it made, and leaves nothing when removed.
- **Web fragment**: The row's chart written as a web fragment by Python draws, once mounted, what the chart of its spec draws, reports the datum under the pointer and a refused payload, and leaves nothing when unmounted.

## Names across surfaces

The API audit (scripts/contracts/api.mjs): every option, event, and string choice of the spec reaches each surface under the name that surface's convention makes of it. The spec, TypeScript, and the adapters spell options in camelCase, Python in snake_case; React's listeners are onSelect, Svelte's onselect, Vue's events select, and the notebook widget's on_select; a handle's getter and setter are the widget's property of their name (getHiddenKeys and setHiddenKeys, hidden_keys), where api.json spells them otherwise (the widget's get_viewport and set_viewport are methods); Python's mark methods split a number from its word (point_3d); and a Vue template's bare attribute (`<Line dodge />`) is true for every option of a mark component that takes true.

Where a surface differs, and why (scripts/contracts/api.mjs checks each), by audit: what it compares, the surface, and the option whose settings it reads, if any (`mark rule python`: the rule mark's options in Python; `mark line python continuity maxGap`: the settings of a line's continuity.maxGap there; `mark * helper`: what every mark's helper shares; `settings ScalePadding hg.Pixels`: a settings type against the Python class taking it).
