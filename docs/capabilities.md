## Placement

- **Named panel**: The row's mark drawn in one of two panels of one size draws what it draws in the other; the line beside it draws as alone; no other panel draws anything. Proved in [placement.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/contracts/placement.test.ts).
- **Facets**: Split by the chart's facet field into panels N and S, each panel draws what the mark draws over that group's rows alone. Proved in [placement.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/contracts/placement.test.ts).
- **Facets in a layout cell**: A layout cell splits the mark's rows into facet panels inside it.
- **Nested layout**: In columns of rows of four panels of one size, the mark draws in its panel what it draws in another, at its place in the nesting; the others draw nothing of it. Proved in [placement.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/contracts/placement.test.ts).
- **Spanning cell**: In a cell spanning both columns above two panels, the mark draws as in the top panel of rows of a panel and a grid of two: across both columns. Proved in [placement.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/contracts/placement.test.ts).
- **Inset**: In one of two insets of one size over a panel, the mark draws what it draws in the other; the panel draws its line as without it. Proved in [placement.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/contracts/placement.test.ts).

## Selection

## Reading

- **Nearest x**: With inspection mode nearest-x, the pointer on a datum reads every series' point at its x: the hover event's points, their labels, rows, and texts, are the fixture's. Annotations of a table along x (rules, text) read their row at its x. Proved in [reading.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/browser/contracts/reading.test.ts).

- **Cursor sync**: Two charts in a cursor group: the pointer on one shows the cursor at that value in the other, though it shows another range (its crosshair at the value's pixel), once and without echo; a channel naming a panel carries that panel's cursor only (a shared proof). - **Viewport sync**: Two charts in a viewport group: a view set on one (setViewport) is the other's at once, along x or, where x shows categories, along y; each reports it once. Proved in [lifecycle.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/browser/contracts/lifecycle.test.ts).
- **Selection sync**: Two charts in a selection group: a range selected on one is the other's selection, in data values, reported once on each. Proved in [lifecycle.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/browser/contracts/lifecycle.test.ts).
- **Hydration**: A chart drawn on the server (renderToSnapshot) is hydrated: its element and geometry nodes are taken over, not written again; it reads its datum, takes an update, and leaves the page when destroyed. Proved in [lifecycle.test.ts](../packages/herceg/test/browser/contracts/lifecycle.test.ts).
